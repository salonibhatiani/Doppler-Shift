Doppler shift of light

A light source emits wavefronts at a fixed frequency. Move it, and the waves bunch up ahead (blueshift) and stretch out behind (redshift). Each ring is colored by the wavelength an observer in that direction would see.

Wave speed on screen = c. Source speed scales with v/c.

Controls

Light sent sideways isn't shifted. Good for a first look.
